# Pitch Pilot

A singing game. Your voice steers a little flyer up and down; sing the right note (do, re, mi, fa, sol, la, ti, do) to fly through the gap in each wall.

Play: https://klarakga-creator.github.io/pitch-pilot/

- Allow the microphone when asked, then tap **Find my key** so the scale fits your voice.
- 12 levels plus Endless, stars, XP ranks and unlockable flyers.
- Zen mode: no walls, just sing.
- Headphones help. Everything runs in your browser; no audio leaves your device.

The whole game is a single `index.html` with no build step.
